Before you start

  1. Have paper, a pen and a calculator ready. Close your notes.
  2. When you press Start, the questions and a timer appear.
  3. Write all your working on paper. Show every step — marks are given for method, not just the answer.
  4. When you finish (or time runs out), take clear photos of your pages and upload them.
  5. Your work is then marked and you get feedback straight away.
